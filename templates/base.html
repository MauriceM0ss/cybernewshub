Auto-refresh

A change takes effect within a minute. Use the ⟳ Refresh button in the header to pull updates right away.

Watchlist

One term per line. Matches are highlighted in article titles and summaries, and flagged with a 👁 marker. CVE IDs are linked automatically.

Pop a desktop notification when a refresh finds new articles matching your watchlist. Works while CyberNewsHub is open in a browser tab; your browser will ask permission once.

AI Daily Brief

Generate a once-a-day summary of what matters across your feeds using the Claude API (the ✦ button in the header). Your key is stored locally on the server, never sent to the browser, and never committed to git.

Keyboard shortcuts

        
Work anywhere in the article list (not while typing in a field or with a
          dialog open).

        

          
j / k
Next / previous article

          
o / Enter
Open the selected article (marks it read)

          
s
Star / unstar (keep permanently)

          
b
Bookmark to read later

          
m / r
Toggle read / unread

          
/
Jump to search

          
Esc

Good to know

        
    
          
  • ⟳ Refresh pulls every feed right away; feeds also auto-refresh on the
            interval set in ⚙ Settings.
    • 
          
  • Watchlist terms (⚙ Settings) are highlighted in articles and flagged with 👁;
            enable desktop notifications there to be alerted on new matches.
    • 
          
  • ★ Starred keeps an article permanently; 🔖 Read later bookmarks it
            for soon — both have their own views in the sidebar and survive cleanup.
    • 
          
  • CVE IDs in article summaries link straight to the NVD entry.
    • 
          
  • ✦ Daily Brief summarises the last 24 hours across your feeds with the Claude API
            — add an API key under ⚙ Settings to enable it.
    • 
          
  • Add feeds, manage categories, import/export OPML and more under ⚙ Settings.
